P2P Logic

What is Peer-to-Peer?

In a traditional model, you upload files to a server (cloud), and then download them. In P2P, devices connect directly. It's faster, private, and has no middleman.

Aether P2P

  • Files stay on your devices - you own your data
  • Completely free - no subscriptions or limits
  • Lightning-fast direct device-to-device transfer
  • Works on local network without internet
  • End-to-end encryption - maximum privacy
