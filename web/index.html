What is Peer-to-Peer?

  • Peer-to-Peer (P2P) technology shifts data transfer from servers to direct device connection, bypassing central vulnerabilities.
  • Aether creates a secure, encrypted tunnel where data flows exclusively between your owned devices.
  • Eliminating the middleman removes file size limits and unlocks maximum transfer speeds on local networks.
  • Complete privacy is guaranteed; your data never resides on a computer you do not own or control.
